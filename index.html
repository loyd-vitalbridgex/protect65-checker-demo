Your deadlines

Special Enrollment Period: December 8, 2026 – February 28, 2027. Because your plan is ending, you get this extra window to enroll in a new Medicare Advantage or Part D plan.

Medigap guaranteed-issue window: You also have a guaranteed right to buy a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy — no health questions, no denial for pre-existing conditions. You can apply starting 60 days before your plan ends, up to 63 days after it ends. Do not let this window close.

Avoid a coverage gap: Enroll in December and your new coverage starts January 1, 2027 — the day after your current plan ends.
